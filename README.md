 Smart Study Assistant is an all-in-one multimodal AI learning platform engineered to transform raw study material into interactive, high-retention knowledge assets. The platform accepts diverse input formats—including PDFs, handwritten notes, whiteboard photos, typed text, live voice recordings, and pre-recorded audio clips—and synthesizes them into personalized study aids.
With cross-lingual intelligence, students can input content in one language and generate outputs in an entirely different language, bridging academic language barriers across all Indian regional languages and major global languages.
Core Features:
 * Universal Multimodal Input
   * Process textbook PDFs, lecture slides, research documents, and handwritten notes.
   * Analyse images, chalkboard photos, and clipboard-pasted screenshots.
   * Transcribe and evaluate live microphone recordings or pre-recorded audio lecture clips.
   * Direct clipboard text and image pasting for instant processing.
 * Automated Active-Recall Study Suite
   * Structured Summaries: Key takeaways, core definitions, and concise bulleted breakdowns.
   * Visual Concept Diagrams: Structural and step-by-step visual representations of complex topics.
   * Adaptive Flashcards: Spaced-repetition-ready cards targeting essential terms, formulas, and facts.
   * Interactive Quizzes: Practice tests and dynamic assessments to evaluate concept mastery.
 * Conversational Two-Host Mini-Podcasts
   * Converts study materials into natural, conversational audio discussions between two AI co-hosts.
   * Fully customizable language output for on-the-go, hands-free auditory learning.
 * AI Oral Viva & Interview Simulator
   * Practice oral examinations and technical interviews with an adaptive AI examiner.
   * Probing follow-up questions, real-time feedback, and session performance tracking.
 * 24/7 Interactive AI Study Tutor
   * Real-time conversational tutor for deep dives, clarifying complex equations, and step-by-step explanations.
 * Cross-Lingual Engine
   * Full input-to-output language decoupling: input lecture notes in one language (e.g., Hindi, Telugu, or Tamil) and generate summaries, quizzes, podcasts, or viva simulations in another (e.g., English, French, or German).
   * Comprehensive coverage across Indian regional languages and global languages.
Target Audience:
 * Students & Exam Aspirants: Streamline semester prep, competitive exams, and viva voce defences.
 * Multilingual Learners: Master academic concepts taught in secondary languages through native-language explanations.
 * Auditory & Visual Learners: Switch effortlessly between interactive quizzes, visual diagrams, and two-host audio podcasts.
 * Educators & Researchers: Rapidly generate lesson overviews, bilingual study aids, and interactive assessments.
